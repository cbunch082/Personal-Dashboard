✦How the Focus Ritual works

The Pomodoro technique works with your ADHD brain — not against it. Short bursts of focus followed by guilt-free breaks give your dopamine system the rhythm it craves.

🔮
Focus session
Work on one thing only. Close other tabs. Let the timer hold you.
🌿
Break
Step away. Stretch. Drink water. Do not keep working — the break is the point.
🖤
After 4 sessions
Take a longer break. 20–30 minutes. You've earned it.
